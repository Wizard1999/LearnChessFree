2026-10-24. **Status of the input:** an AI-written synthesis supplied by Andrew (its links carry `utm_source=chatgpt.com`). It explicitly says the sequencing and standards are its own synthesis. I treat it as a **checklist and a second opinion**, not as evidence about what any book contains. Where it disagrees with a primary text I read, the primary text wins. Labels: **[V]** verified here; **[R]** recalled; **[X]** I think it is wrong or unsupported; **[U]** unverified.

## 2. What the pasted curriculum has that ours lacked (adopted)

- `SAF.DEFEND-OPTIONS`, `END.MATE-STALEMATE-AVOID`, `OPN.PUNISH`, `POS.REASSESS` (above).
- **"Knowledge versus performance"** as an explicit teachable idea: knowing why a move is good does not guarantee finding it under play (Rowson's *Zebras* is the cited source [U]). We cover this through cue level 3–4 evidence; add a short self-direction item that shows the learner their own gap between explaining and finding (`SELF.3`).
- **"Historical development of ideas"** (comparing how generations handled the same problem). Added as a low-priority node `GAME.HISTORY` (T6+), using public-domain games and our own text.
- **Dvoretsky's *Endgame Analysis*** as a training model for independent analysis: confirms our `END.TABLEBASE-LAB` plus written conclusions at T6–7.

## 3. What ours has that the pasted curriculum lacks

- A **single Thinking Loop** across tiers (it has candidate moves and variation trees only at stage 9).
- **Clock literacy and practical performance** before stage 14 (S10 from T1).
- **Cue levels, unlabeled testing standards and contrast families** as the assessment mechanism. Its "advance when…" lines are qualitative ("reliably", "coherent") with no measurable standard.
- **Self-direction from the start** and the independence ledger, silent sessions, confidence calibration.
- **Evidence, calibration and rights discipline**: every claim machine-checked or reviewed, with evidence grades.
- A graph rather than a line: stronger players can repair a basic hole without walking everything below their level.
