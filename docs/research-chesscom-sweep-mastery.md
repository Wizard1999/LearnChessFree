# chess.com sweep: Mastery-only courses (started 9 Nov 2026, segment 7)
Courses whose badge is M only (the newer "Mastery" tag; 248 courses, most of them master-game, opening or Jeremy Kane "Play Like" series). Method as in `research-chesscom-sweep-intermediate.md` (lesson 1, challenge 1, one wrong move where it registers, engine at depth 14 with the local Stockfish 19 lite, labels [V] verified here, [I] inference). Original wording only; short facts, no copied lesson text. Mastery courses that carry an A or I badge as well sit in the advanced and intermediate sweep files.

## Cross-course patterns (Mastery, running list)
(Updated 9 Nov 2026, segment 7, from 15 Mastery samples.) Mastery-only shells mostly give short feedback (40 to 130 characters), a blank prompt, a class hint on a wrong move ("look for a strong capture / check / pawn sacrifice"), and a one-line question after the right move that sets the next challenge. Exceptions: Silman and Shankland write first-person annotations, Wolski writes refutation lines in notation. Claims checked against the engine: three failed (Improve Your Chess "serious difficulties" at +0.11, Build Your Technique "throws away the win" at +1.8, Silman's lessons rejecting the engine's top two), while Mental Domination, Chess Traps, SCC Tactics, Rook and Knight vs Rook were sound. Best formats to borrow: the stalemate trap named in two words (Rook and Knight vs Rook), "Play Against [legend]" as exploiting a champion's slip (Play Against Capablanca), the defensive-resource challenge "how can you stay in the game?" (SCC Tactics), one gambit shown in five games (Gambit Buffet), a trap catalogue indexed by motif (Every Opening Trap), "equally good" wording for accepted alternatives (Defeating Pet Openings).

- Engine [V]: Rh5 +1.42, Qf3 +0.98, Qh5 +0.19, Rh3 -0.35. All three response tiers appeared in one challenge: Rh3 "incorrect: there's a better move; try again and look for a way to target the f5-pawn" (names the target, not the move); Qf3 "is an alternative: reasonable, but it's better to target f5 with another piece. How should you do it?" (the engine gap is 0.44, and the text says which class of move is better); Rh5 "correct: Black can't capture on e4 without allowing mate on the g-file. - Idea [I]: the same concept word ("target f5") appears in the wrong text, the alternative text and the question that follows the right move, so the learner sees one criterion applied three ways. Scholarmate's hint ladder can reuse it: name the target square, then the piece class, then the move. Rapid-chess framing is a label only: nothing in the lesson depends on clocks.
