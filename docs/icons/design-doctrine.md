# Scholarmate icon doctrine — working version 3

This file records the owner's decisions through Batch 02. Explicit later feedback supersedes an earlier prompt. Do not treat generated candidates as approved merely because they exist. Read review-status.md and manifest.json before using a reference.

## Identity

Scholarmate is a present-day, distinguished chess-learning academy with history, culture, curiosity and brave intellectual adventure. Think of a contemporary university with inherited craft and traditions. It is not a fantasy setting, medieval settlement, magical school or steampunk world. Modern familiar objects are welcome. Historical character should not force every object to be antique brass, damaged or old.

The rendering influence is the warm, naturalistic hand-painted quality of Ghibli animated films: things feel convincing and alive through drawing, color and selective detail. Warcraft informs small-size icon readability and strong identity. These influences do not authorize game-world branding, copied compositions, characters, UI frames or fantasy props everywhere.

## Three separate decisions

1. Subject: a concrete recognizable object, action or force that suggests a useful idea.
2. Rendering: richly but delicately painted; visible designed color masses, believable form, controlled detail.
3. Presentation: a black pocket of space and the separate approved bookbinding frame.

Changing subject from lantern to electric lightbulb must not change rendering from illustration to product photography. The rejected bulb demonstrates this exact failure.

## Visual language

Use evocative physical metaphors, not little chess diagrams or repeated chess-piece arrangements. A pin can be a real fastening pin; skewering should be recognizable as an action. An image should suggest an idea and feeling before its learned chess meaning. Reuse an image only for closely related meanings. Maintain a separate plan for twelve dedicated portraits: six piece types in each color.

A visual can succeed while its assigned meaning fails. The wheel wedge reads immediately as a wedged wheel, but the owner rejected its assignment to blockade. Preserve it as a good unassigned asset. Do not reassign it silently. The hook with two colored rocks fails both immediate meaning and small-size recognition; replace the metaphor rather than endlessly polishing it.

## Rendering balance

- Strong silhouette and a few broad value/color masses must read at 32px.
- Fine texture, scratches, chipped paint, grain and craftsmanship reward larger viewing where appropriate.
- Avoid both coarse faceted impasto and sterile smooth simplified surfaces.
- Avoid photo-like glass, glossy slick food, dense microtexture and blurry photographic lighting.
- Preserve painted color shapes and confident drawing. Texture must not obscure the main form.
- Ordinary things can be well maintained. Do not cover every surface in cracks, rust or battle damage.
- Subject-specific rich colors are independent of the website skin. Do not impose a brand palette on the objects.
- One main subject. Additional objects must be essential to the action and remain legible.
- No text, labels, logos, background scenes, checkerboards or decorative clutter.

The approved shield and the approved pin are the strongest rendering references. The approved book, compass, magnifier and weight supply subject-specific successes; do not infer that their most photographic details should be exaggerated on future assets.

## Background and light

Each object is seen through a small square window into black space. Default background: deep black. No automatic colored backdrop.

No atmosphere is needed when silhouette and illumination already separate the subject. Optional atmosphere is a faint, uneven, diffuse patch BEHIND an object, mostly occluded, rapidly fading into black. It must not trace the outline or illuminate non-emissive material. A luminous contour, neon ring or evenly encircling aura is rejected.

An actual light-emitting subject may illuminate itself and nearby space. It still needs the same painted rendering and restrained composition. Neither a lightbulb nor modernity implies photography.

## Frame

Place the entire square artwork inside the frame, inset 6.25% on each side. The border surrounds the art and never overlaps its four edges. Only tiny rounded image corners may be clipped. Use frames/frame.svg: the approved clean bookbinding design. Leather-like edge, rounded corners and fine gilt rules. No leaves, corner flourishes, heraldry or details intruding into icon space. Apply it after image generation outside the artwork as a reusable frame; never bake it into a generated master. The frame is independent of site theme.

## Approved pin invariant

The first pin's cloth and fastening geometry are specifically preferred. The latest approved version changes the original blue background to black. Preserve that exact pin, cloth folds, head, shaft, tip, brush treatment and composition. Do not substitute a more realistic coarse-weave dangling ribbon or change how the pin passes through the fabric.

## Use in the school

Use concept identities in lesson lists, topic headers, search, recap and post-game links. In unlabelled retrieval exercises, hide topic icons until feedback; they can leak which technique is relevant. Keep standard small operational controls simple. Icons supplement text and do not replace accessible labels.
