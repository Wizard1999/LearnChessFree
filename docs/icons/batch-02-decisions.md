## General production corrections

1. Never regenerate an approved asset to complete a batch count. Copy the approved master unchanged.
2. More readable does not automatically mean shorter, thicker, simplified or distorted. Preserve elegant proportions; improve value contrast and secondary detail first.
3. Material identity matters: glass must read as glass, not opaque yellow. Paint reflections without reverting to photo-like density.
4. A switched-on bulb emits light. This differs from an arbitrary neon outline around a non-emissive object.
5. All connected mechanical components must share coherent axes and perspective. Check skewer collinearity and bulb filament/base orientation.
6. Books are slim rectangular solids: two aligned covers around a matching page block. Natural packing matters as much as local geometry.
7. Avoid repeated arbitrary blue ribbons. Accessories must earn their place and are not a universal style signature.
