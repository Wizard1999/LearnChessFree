# Play culture (D79)

The goal is a habit, not a feature: always be playing, and always bring one recent lesson into the game and think about it afterwards. We are not a play platform, so real people are met elsewhere.

What exists [V: built and tested]:
- After a practice game: a "Keep playing" note, and "Which of your recent lessons showed up in this game?" with four tags per lesson, saved on this device as a reflection (shown in the Learning record notes).
- Once any game exists: "Play a game today" with "What to try this game" on Today and in the Whole curriculum card. The line only names the learner's most recent finished lesson.
- `#/people`, Play real people: Lichess and Chess.com, with what each offers, safety notes for beginners and minors, the page each fact came from and what was not checked.
- My games leads with "Review a game you played elsewhere" (paste PGN or fetch by username), so online games feed the learning loop.

Rules kept: no live AI in the learner's loop, no accounts or sign-in of ours, nothing sent to the outside sites (plain links, new tab, no referral or tracking parameters), every statement about a site read on its own page on 9 November 2026 and labelled, unchecked points listed.

Not done [H]: measuring whether the nudge changes how often people play (feedback task after release); adding more sites (only after their pages are read); showing the tags per game inside My games.
