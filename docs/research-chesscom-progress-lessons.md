## Recipe to rebuild any course's lesson list (run in the browser console on any chess.com page)
Fetch `/lessons/<slug>` with `credentials:'include'`, parse with DOMParser, collect `main a` hrefs matching `/lessons/<slug>/<lesson-slug>`; the card text holds "N min" and "N Challenges". The same loop over `/lessons/all-lessons?page=1..17` (anchors `/lessons/<slug>`) lists the 395 courses. Both pages are the ones a learner sees; nothing from locked lesson data is read.
