## Where Scholarmate is now [V from this repo, 29 October 2026]
204-skill prerequisite graph; 159 lessons covering 162 nodes; about 6,700 exercises, every claim checked by rules, engine or tablebase; unlabelled testing; evidence-based mastery with refreshers; missions map; placement and warm-ups; contrast lab; game review with detectors; openings explorer built from 4.75 million games; famous-games course (8); free and open; runs offline in one portable exe; human review and feedback tools built; no account. **Not yet:** a strong player has reviewed nothing (all lessons needs-human); no testers or evidence of learning gains; no sign-in sync; no video; no live coach; 42 of 204 nodes have no lesson.

## Where it will be when the roadmap is complete [I: plans, not facts]
Every node taught once, with links instead of overlap; the first 10 hours reviewed by strong players; opening deep dives, history and playstyles courses; accessibility pass; evaluation done with real learners; badges and certificates. It would still have no video, no live play, no AI coach.

## Honest comparison
1. **Where we are ahead or distinctive:** one unified curriculum with nothing taught twice; every position machine-checked; tests that do not name the idea; honest, evidence-based progress; free forever; works offline; teaches how to study. No product I found combines these [I: from the search results only].
2. **Where competitors are ahead today:** master-written and human-reviewed content (Chessable, Chess.com); play and community (Lichess, Chess.com); personalisation from your own games by AI (the app category above); polish and mobile apps; proof that learners improve (we have none yet).
3. **After the roadmap:** content breadth should be competitive for beginners to club level; the gaps remain human expertise (needs reviewers), personalisation from your own games (we have game review, not an AI coach), mobile apps, and above all evidence. The case for it rests on the first 10 hours being excellent and measured.
4. **The risk the AI coach apps pose:** they promise a personal coach for $8 a month. Our answer is trust (verified, reviewed, no hallucinated advice) and teaching the learner to study independently, which we have to prove, not claim.
