# Free forever

Scholarmate is made by Andrew Valentine and is meant to stay free, in two senses:

1. **Free of cost.** Anyone may use it, host it, copy it and change it, at no charge, for learning and teaching chess.
2. **Free of sale.** It may not be sold, or included in anything sold or paid for, including subscriptions, paid apps, courses behind a paywall or products that charge for access to it. Advertising-funded copies are also outside the intent.

Andrew keeps control of the project for one purpose only: to keep these two promises true for the public, for as long as the project exists. This pledge is the reason for the licence choices in `LICENSES.md`, and any change of licence or hosting must keep it.

Credit is always required: "Created by Andrew Valentine" (see `NOTICE`).
